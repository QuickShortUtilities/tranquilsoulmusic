Terms of use

The important part is the licence: on the Creator plan, what you generate is yours to use commercially, permanently.

Subscriptions

  • Free — three generated pieces per day, for personal listening.
  • Plus — unlimited generation, for personal listening.
  • Creator — unlimited generation, plus the commercial licence below and access to the full catalogue.

Subscriptions are billed monthly through your Apple account and renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel any time in Settings; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Where a free trial is offered, cancelling before it ends means you are not charged.

Licence for what you generate

On the Creator plan, music you generate is licensed to you for commercial use, worldwide, in perpetuity — and stays licensed even if you later cancel.

That covers social video, YouTube, podcasts, client work, advertising, apps and installations. We will not file a copyright claim against content you publish using it.

On the Free and Plus plans, generated music is for personal listening only.

You may not resell or redistribute the music as music — as a stock library, a sample pack, a beat store, or as training data for another model. The licence is for using it in something, not for selling it as something.

How the music is made

LORNA is trained solely on recordings written and owned by Tranquil Soul Music. No third-party material and no scraped datasets. That is the reason we are able to license output to you at all, and it is why the commercial licence carries no third-party risk.

Fair use of the service

Unlimited plans are subject to a generous soft cap that exists only to catch automated abuse. Ordinary use will never reach it. If it is reached we will contact you before restricting anything.
