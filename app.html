Trained on our own work

LORNA learned from 5,500 recordings we wrote and own outright. No scraped data, no borrowed style — which is the only reason a commercial licence is possible at all.

Six dials, real DSP

Atmosphere, tempo, warmth, bass, melody and rhythm each drive a live node in the audio chain — reverb size, playback rate, spectral tilt, delay. Move one and you hear it immediately.

Contexts, not genres

Sleep, Unwind, Focus, Gym, Sauna, Recover. Each is a target in the same parameter space, so a preset and a hand-tuned soundscape are the same kind of thing.

Reads the room

Suggests a context from the time of day and, with permission, your heart-rate variability against your own 30-day baseline. It tells you why, and you can always override it.

Apple Health

Listening and breathing sessions write to Health as mindful minutes, so they count alongside everything else you track.

Breathing & layers

Guided patterns — 5-5, box, 4-7-8 — plus six ambient stems you can layer over anything playing.

Works offline

Everything you generate is cached. Playlists, favourites and downloads are free, forever.
