Separately collectable

Publishing is a second cheque.

Distributors pay the recording. Songwriting royalties are collected by a different body entirely, and are the most commonly missed money in independent catalogues.

PRS for Music

Performance royalties — streaming, radio, and anywhere the music plays in public. Roughly 10–15% on top of the master side. If the catalogue was never registered, this has gone uncollected.

MCPS / mechanical

Mechanical royalties on streams and downloads. Usually collected alongside PRS in the UK.

YouTube Content ID

Pays when other people use the music. For meditation and ambient this is often larger than the artist's own channel — and it collects nothing unless the catalogue is claimed.

Rates are 2026 blended estimates and vary by territory, subscriber mix and deal. Treat the output as an order of magnitude, not an invoice.
