Privacy

Short version: the music you make stays on your device and our render service. We do not sell anything about you, and Health data never leaves your phone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Pieces you generate, your playlists and favourites.
  • Session history and total mindful minutes.
  • Your soundscape settings and preferences.

All of this lives in the app's own storage. It is removed when you delete the app.

Apple Health

Health data never leaves your device. If you turn on Apple Health, the app writes completed sessions as Mindful Minutes and reads heart-rate variability to suggest a listening context. Both happen entirely on your phone.

We do not transmit, store or receive any Health data, and we never use it for advertising, marketing or product recommendations. Shop suggestions are based on the context you selected, never on anything read from Health.

What the render service receives

When you generate a piece, the app sends the settings needed to render it: the context, the six parameters, the length, and a seed. It also sends an install identifier so we can count renders against your daily allowance, and — if you subscribe — Apple's signed receipt so we know which plan you are on.

That identifier is a random value tied to the app install. It is not your name, email, device ID or Apple account, and it resets if you reinstall.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We receive only the signed receipt confirming which plan is active.

The website

The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If you join the mailing list we store your address to send the newsletter, and nothing else. Unsubscribe is one click and removes it.

Radio streams are served over standard HTTP requests; our host logs IP addresses briefly for abuse prevention, as any web server does.

The shop

Partner items are sold by the partner on their own site under their own privacy policy. We may earn a commission. We do not pass them anything about you.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask what we hold, ask for it corrected, or ask for it deleted. Email relax@tranquilsoulmusic.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app and by email before it takes effect.
